# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to an open, welcoming community. Participation must be free of harassment,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.
We aim to make participation respectful, inclusive, and safe for everyone.

## Our standards

Helpful behavior includes empathy, respect for different experiences, constructive feedback,
accepting responsibility for mistakes, and considering the needs of the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment; sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention;
trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks; publishing private information without
permission; threats; and other conduct inappropriate in a professional community.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers clarify and enforce these standards fairly. They may remove or edit
contributions that violate this code and explain moderation decisions when appropriate.
Enforcement should protect people affected by misconduct and avoid unnecessary disclosure
of their identities or reports.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone represents the project publicly, including
through an official account, appointed role, or project event.

## Reporting

Report concerns to [info@zenitgroup.com.co](mailto:info@zenitgroup.com.co).
Include enough context to investigate. Reports will be reviewed with care and respect for the
privacy and safety of everyone involved. Report a conflict involving a maintainer through the
same address and identify the conflict so it can be handled by an uninvolved representative
where possible.

## Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction:** For inappropriate language or other unprofessional behavior, a private
   explanation of the violation and the expected correction; a public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning:** For a violation through a single incident or a series of actions, a warning
   with consequences for continued behavior and, where needed, a period without contact with
   affected people or interaction in relevant project spaces.
3. **Temporary ban:** For a serious violation or sustained inappropriate behavior, a temporary
   ban from community participation and contact with affected people for a specified period.
4. **Permanent ban:** For persistent violations, harassment, aggression, or discriminatory
   conduct, permanent exclusion from community spaces.

Responses depend on impact and context. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern
or participates in an investigation is itself a violation.
